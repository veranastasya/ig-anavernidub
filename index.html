What the numbers say

23,200 views, 6,355 people reached, and the follower count did not move. It sat at 2,090 yesterday and it sits at 2,090 today. Three follows came from these five posts. Reach more than doubled week over week and interactions tripled, so the distribution is working — but almost none of it is converting into anyone choosing to hear from you again. That gap, not the view count, is the number to fix.

Two different machines, and you are running both. Reels reached 83–86% non-followers; carousels reached 4–5%. Reels are how strangers find you, carousels are how existing followers stay warm. Judging a carousel by views, or a Reel by engagement rate, will make you optimise the wrong thing — the Carrie Bradshaw post has the best engagement rate of the week at 21.8% and produced zero new followers.

Yesterday's Reel tells you what a one-push post looks like. The 6am workout opened at 2,086 views in four hours and added just 366 in the following twenty-four. No shares, no saves, nothing to carry it into a second wave. Compare the "I LOVE New York, BUT" Reel, which had 5 shares and is still climbing on day three. Shares are what buy a post a second life; today's Netflix Reel has 3 of them already, which is the thing to watch as it matures.

Saves are still effectively zero. Four saves across five creatives. Saves are the strongest signal that something is worth returning to, and nothing you published qualified — including an NYC itinerary, the exact format people normally save. Last week the one post that produced real acquisition (the DMV gym roundup: 5 follows, 4 saves) was the one that gave someone something to keep. It has now rolled out of the 7-day window, and with it went most of your follows.

Stories remain a large, barely-measured channel. 46% of views and 43% of interactions came from Stories, not from anything in the grid above.

Nothing this week pointed at Wellio. Five creatives, all lifestyle and travel, one of them AI-generated comedy. As a reach week it worked. As funnel-building for a client-management platform for coaches it did nothing, and the flat follower count means it did not even bank an audience for the lifestyle content. The format that converted last week — a useful, specific, save-worthy roundup — is the same format that would work for coaching content. That is the experiment worth running next.
