What the numbers say

Reach collapsed and the account got better. Views down 69%, reach down 77% from the viral week — and profile visits hit 374, the highest figure this dashboard has ever recorded, beating 323 from late August when reach was a twentieth of today’s. 1.76% of people reached opened the profile, against 0.30% a week ago. Followers up 8 on a fifth of the audience.

The best-converting post in the whole dataset is from this week. "Your muscles went on a vacay around week 3" reached 2,298 people and produced 4 follows: one per 575. Nothing else comes close — REVD managed one per 1,110, the Sep 18 Italy Reel one per 1,167, the 81,586-reach Reel one per 5,099. It is a joke that carries a real training idea, at a size Instagram was happy to distribute. That is the construction to run again.

Five posts, all about training, and all four Reels reached strangers at roughly 90%. This is the first week where the whole slate distributed rather than one post carrying the account. The fitness subject is not the obstacle it looked like three weeks ago — the obstacle was the explainer shape, and the jokes have now carried the same material past it.

The Wellio mention is worth watching. The Oct 21 Reel is the first to name the product, and it finished at 1.3% engagement with no shares and no follows, against 2.7%, 4.1% and 4.9% for its neighbours on comparable reach. One post proves nothing. Two more will.

What that means for the next post. Run the Oct 19 construction again: one observation about training that is true and slightly funny, no instruction, no product. Then look at the carousel — "one small thing has stopped moving" was advice written as a paragraph and it reached 344 people with From Other of 1, while the carousels that worked were lists of named things with prices. The format is not the problem; naming things is what gets carousels moved.
