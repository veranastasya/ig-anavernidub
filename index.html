What the numbers say

The recommendation this dashboard has been making for three weeks just failed its first real test. The Oura Ring post was exactly the brief: a specific, useful, niche take with a real question in the caption and topical hashtags. It reached 415 accounts — the smallest of anything in the window — and 100% of its interactions came from people who already follow you. No shares, no saves, and no answers to the question it asked. Meanwhile a five-second night-out clip captioned "Things were discussed" is two hours old and already holds the records for shares (7), engagement per reach (6.7%) and non-follower engagement (75%).

What that probably means. Distribution is decided in the first seconds, before the caption matters. The posts that travel here are visual and immediately legible — the Netflix joke, the Pilates hook, this clip. The Oura post opens on a talking head explaining a considered opinion, which is a format Instagram tests narrowly and, in this case, stopped testing. The written substance was good; it never got shown to anyone who could have valued it. That is a delivery problem, not a topic problem, and the fix is the first two seconds, not the idea.

The account-level picture has not improved. Views 22,261 (+7%) but interactions down 34% and followers down to 2,106, five below the Aug 25 peak. Posting resumed after the two-day gap, which stopped the view decline, but nothing has reversed the engagement trend. Non-follower interactions fell from 26.9% to 22.8%.

Still one converter. Four weeks of data, and the boosted carousel remains the only post that produced followers at any rate — 8 organic follows off ~348 people. Across the same window five Reels reached roughly 9,900 accounts and produced three followers, two of them from the Pilates post.

What is worth trying next. The Oura idea is not dead; its packaging is. The same content as a carousel — the comparison as slides rather than as narration — puts the useful part in the format that has actually converted here, and removes the dependence on holding attention through a spoken intro. If a second talking-head explainer also stalls at ~400 reach, that settles it: this account can distribute visual hooks and convert carousels, and video explainers are not a format it can currently get shown.
