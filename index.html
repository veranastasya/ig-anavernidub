What the numbers say

Publishing six times reversed everything. Views up 30%, interactions up 87%, profile visits up 32%, followers up three after a week of minus one. Last week’s read-out said frequency was the constraint, not reach. This is the fourth appearance of that pattern and the first time it has run in the positive direction. Treat it as settled: post most days or the account decays within 48 hours.

And the conversion problem got worse, not better. The gym Reel is the largest thing this account has produced in a month — 3,600 strangers, 68 shares, 344 interactions — and Instagram credits it with zero follows and zero profile visits. That is five Reels in a row where large reach produced nothing. Meanwhile the only follow recorded in eight days came from the wellness-costs Reel of Sep 1, off 395 people. Across this account, reach and conversion are now inversely related, which means chasing views is actively spending attention you cannot bank.

The HYROX carousel is the failure worth studying. It was the right shape on paper: a specific, named, searchable thing, explained. It is what the gym-roundup carousel did when it pulled 5 follows off 831 reach. Instead 96.4% of its views came from people who already follow you, From Other reached only 153, and not one person saved it. A "what is this thing" explainer that earns zero saves has not answered a question anyone was actually asking. The subject was right; the post did not carry information a stranger needed.

One thing is quietly working. The Sep 7 Friday-energy Reel is at 71.6% non-follower views with seven comments and three saves after one day — the only wellness post this week that both escaped your follower base and got saved. It has a question hook about the viewer, a split-screen visual, and no personal anecdote. That is a different construction from the wellness Reels that stalled at 400 reach, and it is the one to copy.

What that means for the next post. Keep the cadence — that argument is over. Stop spending Reels on trending-audio memes and generic AI news; they buy views this account cannot convert. The next three should follow the Friday-energy construction: a question about the viewer’s own week, a visual that changes, and one specific claim they can act on. And if a post is meant to bring followers rather than views, the evidence says it needs to be saveable — every follow this account has earned came from something worth keeping.
