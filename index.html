What the numbers say

The Pilates post is compounding, and nothing else on this account ever has. Overnight: views up 48%, shares 2 to 6, saves 2 to 4, follows 0 to 2. Every one of those is a forwarding signal, which is why it is still growing while the two comedy Reels have flatlined. Six shares is the most of any creative in the window. It is a day old and has already passed the 6am workout Reel, which is five.

Look at who is engaging with it. 61.8% of its interactions come from non-followers, up from 48.9% last night. The next best post on this page is 33.3%. Strangers are not just being served this Reel, they are acting on it — and the account-level number moved with it: non-follower interactions are 26.9% this week, up from 20.1%, the highest this dashboard has recorded.

The account-level picture is still reach up, engagement down. Views 24,743 (+36%), interactions 587 (−23%), accounts engaged at 3.0% of reach. Reels are now 69.1% of views. The Netflix Reel closed at 3,135 people reached and one follower, which remains the clearest illustration on this page of distribution that banks nothing.

Two formats have now shown they can convert, and they are not the ones getting the reach. The boosted carousel produced 8 organic follows off ~331 people. The Pilates Reel produced 2 follows off 1,650 in its first day and is still moving. Between them: a specific, useful point of view aimed at a niche, and a format people can act on. The comedy Reels reached three times as many people and produced one follower each.

Nothing new went up today. That is worth noting given the Pilates post is the first thing here with real momentum — the moment to publish a follow-up in the same vein is while that audience is still warm and the algorithm is still testing you with strangers. The obvious next test remains the one the last two runs pointed at: the same industry-commentary angle, in carousel form, with a question in the caption.
