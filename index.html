What the numbers say

The account went properly viral and gained ten followers. 93,565 people reached, nearly three times the Europe-trip record, 8,851 interactions, 1,916 shares on one Reel. Net follower change: +10, with 19 follows attributed to posts. This is the same result as Sep 16, at three times the scale.

The conversion rate fell as the reach rose, and the numbers are now unambiguous. The Oct 16 Reel converted one follower per 5,099 people reached. The Sep 18 Italy Reel managed one per 1,167, the REVD studio Reel one per 1,110, and the Hailey Bieber post this week one per 1,254. Reach on this account is not a step toward followers; past a few thousand people it actively works against conversion, because the bigger the audience the less it has to do with you.

Profile visits are the clearest evidence. 93,565 people saw something this week and 278 opened the profile — 0.3%. Through September that figure ran at about 1%. Three times the audience produced a third of the curiosity per person. These are not people who want to know who made it.

The fitness-advice posts are still not being distributed, and one of them deserved better. The Hailey Bieber Reel is the best-constructed advice post you have made: a named person, a genuine question, a clear position. It reached 1,254 people and got two shares. Thirteen advice-shaped posts have now landed between 380 and 1,300 people regardless of how well they are written. The ceiling is in the shape, not the writing.

One thing to be careful about. The Oct 16 Reel carried a follow-ask caption, and so did the Oct 14 Reel two days earlier, which reached 459 people and produced nothing. The caption is not what happened. Something about that specific video travelled, and the account cannot yet reproduce it on purpose — same as the Sep 18 Reel, which also never repeated.

What that means for the next post. There are now roughly 90,000 people who have seen your face this month and 2,148 who follow you. Chasing another viral Reel does not close that gap; two of them have proved it. The useful move is the opposite one: make the next post worth following for the small, interested audience you already have, and judge it on saves, comments and profile visits rather than on reach. The Hailey Bieber construction is the right one — it just needs to be in a shape Instagram will carry.
