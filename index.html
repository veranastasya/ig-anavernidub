What the numbers say

A correction to last week’s read-out. On Oct 25 this page recorded 374 profile visits, called it an all-time high, and read it as the small week outperforming the viral one. That was wrong. Account-level profile visits count traffic arriving in the window regardless of when the post went up, so most of those 374 were the tail of the Oct 16 Reel still being served. With that tail gone, profile visits are 140. The honest version: the viral Reel produced most of the profile traffic after all, just on a delay.

Followers went down 3. 2,156 to 2,153, with two follows attributed to posts. The first decline since the start of October, and it follows the pattern the dashboard has recorded all autumn: the account gains during a reach event and gives some back once it passes.

The split between the two kinds of post is now very clean. The two jokes went out at 92% and 96% non-follower reach. The two posts that explain something — tracking progress, and the yoga intensive — reached 667 and 497 people, and the yoga one sent only 49% of its views to strangers, the lowest share of any Reel this month. Fifteen explainer-shaped posts have now landed under 1,300 people. There is no remaining ambiguity about this.

The tracking idea has now been tried twice and stalled twice. Oct 21 reached 2,571 at 1.3% engagement; Oct 27 reached 667 at 3.6%. Both were about logging your training, which is the Wellio idea. It may be the subject, it may be the construction, but it is not working in this form and the second data point says so.

What that means for the next post. The Oct 29 Reel is doing in two hours what the explainers never managed in six days, so keep making those. But the thing this page has been pointing at for a month is still unaddressed: reach events do not leave followers behind, and the profile is where they are lost. 1,024 people opened it in the last 30 days. Nothing in the content plan changes that number.
