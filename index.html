What the numbers say

Read the account numbers with the ad in mind. Profile visits are up 63% and the follower count finally moved (+18 to 2,108) — but a boost has been running on the Aug 23 carousel since Sunday, and it contributed 36 profile visits and 5 follows for $10.28. The underlying organic trend is real but smaller than the headline. Everything below separates the two.

Reach went up 29% and interactions went down 19%. That is the single most important line on this page. Reels now carry 66.5% of views, up from 47% three weeks ago, and for the first time most of the account's views are from non-followers (55.1%). You are reaching many more strangers and fewer of them are doing anything. Accounts engaged fell to 3.1% of reach.

Three Reels reached 7,700 people between them and produced three followers. One each. The boosted carousel reached 807 and produced thirteen — eight of them organic. The Reels are excellent distribution and almost worthless conversion; the carousel is the reverse. Nothing in six weeks of this dashboard has contradicted that split, and it is now stark enough to act on rather than note.

The Netflix Reel answered its own question. It held past 24 hours where the 6am workout did not — 4,868 views and the widest reach the account has had (3,131). The difference was shares: 5 versus 0. Shares buy a post a second wave; the 6am workout had no forwarding mechanism and added 122 views in four days. If a Reel has not picked up shares in its first day, it is finished.

Saves are still the missing signal. Ten saves across the window and five of them were paid. The only organic saves came from the two Reels that were already travelling. Nothing you published was worth keeping.

Something changed this morning. The Pilates post is the first piece in this window aimed at the industry rather than the lifestyle — a take on a studio's 24/7 virtual reformer model, which is exactly the "builder who talks to coaches" position. It is six minutes old, so there is nothing to judge yet. But it is the first content in six weeks of this dashboard that a wellness coach would have a reason to follow you for, and the format that has been converting all along is the carousel with a question in it. Those two facts point at the same experiment: a specific, useful, save-worthy carousel about the coaching business. Everything else here is buying reach you are not banking.
