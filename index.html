What the numbers say

The growth run stopped. +14, then +11, now −2. Views down 22%, interactions down 31%, and reach off the 32,000 plateau to 28,700. Six follows are attributed to these posts, so roughly eight accounts left. Two good weeks, then a reversal — this is the first evidence of what the travel audience does once the novelty of the trip wears off.

The best post of the week is the smallest one, and nobody saw it. "Your vacation doesn’t end at hotel checkout" reached 763 people and produced a follow — one per 763, four times better than Rome’s one per 4,218 and the best rate recorded here this month. It is also the only post in the window that states something a person could actually do. That is now the fifth week running where the useful post converts several times better per person than the post that travels, and it keeps happening at a scale too small to matter.

"Most effective" is the clearest waste on the page. 2,558 strangers reached, 47 interactions, no saves, no follows, 1.8% engagement. Instagram gave it distribution and the audience gave it nothing back. A Reel that reaches thousands and holds no one is worse than a small post, because it teaches the ranking system that your content does not hold attention.

Carousels are finished as a discovery format here. Florence closed with From Other = 1 — a third consecutive carousel seen by essentially no one outside your followers. Whatever you publish as a carousel now reaches about 300 people, all of whom already follow you. That is a fine tool for talking to your own audience and useless for anything else.

What that means for the next post. The trip is over and the travel run has started decaying on its own, so there is nothing left to protect by staying in it. The wellness carousel is the right next move, provided it is understood as a message to the 2,136 people who already follow you — it will not reach strangers. The piece that has to carry reach is the Reel that follows it, and the register that works is the one in "Six pack has left the chat" and the Rome Reel: a visual joke about the body that reads with the sound off. The failure mode to avoid is another "most effective" — distribution with nothing inside it.
