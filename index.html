What the numbers say

The travel lift is going away. Reach has halved in a week, from 28,734 to 13,000, and is now a third of the Sep 16 peak. Views are down 38%, profile visits down 46%. Followers held at +3, so the audience is not leaving — but the free distribution the trip bought is expiring on schedule, and what is left is an account with 2,139 followers and no travel to post about.

A correction to last week’s read-out. This page said carousels were finished as a discovery format, on the evidence of Florence closing at From Other = 1. The "when in Rome" carousel this week recorded From Other = 316, and the wellness carousel 197. Three bad posts in a row were not the format dying; they were three bad posts. Carousels still reach roughly 400 people, but they are being shown outside the home feed again.

The wellness carousel is the one to learn from, and it did not work. 354 people, zero comments, zero saves, 10.5% engagement — against 22.7% for a casual "when in Rome" carousel posted three days later to a near-identical audience. It was distributed (From Other 197); it simply did not earn a reaction. A post built on personal numbers with no comments and no saves has not given the reader anything to do with it.

Reach and conversion are still unrelated. The Oct 3 Reel put a video in front of 6,897 strangers, collected 75 shares, and produced no follows and no profile visits. The one follow of the week came from the Reel about strength training, at half the reach. That is now the eighth consecutive week this page has recorded the same relationship.

What that means for the next post. Reach is reverting to roughly where it was before the trip, so the question is no longer how to get seen but what to be seen doing. The Oct 6 Reel is the most interesting signal available: 14.2% engagement and nine comments in 90 minutes, which is a conversation starting rather than a video being watched. If that holds through Thursday, it is a better template than anything from the travel run, because comments are the one thing the big Reels never generated.
